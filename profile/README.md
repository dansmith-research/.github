## Dan Smith · plant phenomics research
 
Research code from Dr Dan Smith, plant phenomics researcher at the University of Queensland (Gatton) and research lead of the [APPN-UQ](https://github.com/APPN-UQ) node of the Australian Plant Phenomics Network.
 
I work on getting reliable crop traits out of sensors: UAV photogrammetry, hyperspectral and multispectral remote sensing, thermal imaging, LiDAR and computer vision, mostly for breeding and crop physiology.
 
**This org** holds my own research tools and paper code. Node pipelines and client work live in [APPN-UQ](https://github.com/APPN-UQ).
 
### Tools and projects
| | |
|---|---|
| **CropTraitCV Atlas** | A database of published computer vision models for Australian crop traits, tracking whether trained weights and labelled datasets are actually available |
| **FlightWindow** | How often can you actually fly a given drone sensor at a given place? Odds of a flyable day by month, from 15 years of hourly weather plus sun geometry |
| **biomasspred** | Sensor-agnostic UAV biomass prediction with grouped cross-validation, implementing the method in Smith et al. (2024) *Plant Methods* |
| **Teaching** | Demos and material for crop physiology and phenomics lectures |
